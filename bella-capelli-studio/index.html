What we do

A studio built around the appointment, not the queue.

You sit down with the person cutting your hair, talk through what you want, and leave with something you can manage at home the next morning.

Cuts

Precision cutting for short, mid-length and long hair, shaped to how it falls day to day.

Colour

Full colour, root touch-ups and lightening, matched to your skin tone rather than a swatch book.

Styling

Blow-dries and finishing for an occasion, or a lesson in how to get the same result yourself.

Consultation

Not sure what suits you? Start with a conversation before anything is cut or coloured.

The difference

A small studio means the person who answers the phone is the person doing your hair.

That is the whole idea. There is no front desk passing messages, no rotating chair, and no pressure to add something you did not ask for. You get a clear price and an honest opinion about what will work with your hair.

Appointments are by arrangement, so call ahead rather than dropping in.

Services

What you can book.

Every service starts with a short conversation about your hair, so the result suits you and holds up between visits.

Cut & finish

A cut shaped to your hair and routine, finished and styled so you can see how it sits. We will show you what to do at home.

Colour

Full colour, root retouch or lightening. We talk through the upkeep and the cost before we start, because colour is a commitment, not a one-off.

Highlights

Foil work for brightness and dimension, placed to grow out well rather than leaving a hard line.

Blow-dry & styling

Smooth, volume or waves, for a wedding, a shoot or a night out.

Consultation

A proper look at your hair and an honest recommendation. Useful before a big change, or if a previous colour needs correcting.

Prices depend on hair length, density and the amount of product needed, so they are confirmed with you before any work begins.
