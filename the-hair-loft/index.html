Booking notes

Colour appointments take longer than cuts, so mention colour when you call and we will block out the right amount of time.

If you are coming in for a correction or a big change, a photo helps. Bring one on your phone.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is the safest way to get the time you want. Call the salon and we will find a slot that fits.

How do I know what cut suits me?

That is what the consultation at the start of the appointment is for. Bring a photo or two and we will be straight with you about what will work with your hair.

What should I bring to a colour appointment?

A photo of the tone you are after, and a rough idea of how often you want to come back. Both change what we recommend.

Can you fix a colour I am not happy with?

Often, yes. Call and describe what happened, and we will tell you honestly what can be done in one appointment and what needs two.
