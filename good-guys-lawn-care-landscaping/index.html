Questions

Before you call

How often do you mow?

Most Gainesville yards are on a weekly schedule through the growing season and every other week when growth slows. We set the cadence when we first walk the property.

Do you take away the clippings and debris?

Yes. Cleanup work and hedge trims include hauling the debris off site.

Which parts of town do you cover?

We work across Gainesville and the wider Alachua County area. Call and we will confirm your address is on our route.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can reach the yard, we can work. We will let you know before the first visit so you know what to expect.
