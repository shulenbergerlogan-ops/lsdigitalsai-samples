Why call us

A small problem caught at a checkup is a filling. Left alone, it becomes a bigger appointment.

Most people come in when something hurts, and by then the easy fix has usually passed. A six month checkup catches decay while it is still small, keeps gum problems from taking hold, and means the bigger restorative work can be planned rather than rushed. We take the time to explain what we are seeing and what your options are, so you can decide without pressure.
