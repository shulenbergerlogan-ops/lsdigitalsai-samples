Why call us

A car that is making a noise will not fix itself, and waiting usually makes it dearer.

Most of what we see has been getting worse for a while. A brake that squeals, a check light that has been on for a month, a transmission that slips on the way up a hill: each one is a cheaper repair now than it will be after a breakdown. We drive the vehicle, work out what is actually failing, and tell you what it needs before anything is ordered or taken apart.
