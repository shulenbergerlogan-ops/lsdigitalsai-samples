01 / What we take on

The jobs that need doing

No job is too small to be worth a phone call. If you are not sure whether it is an electrician's job, call and ask, and you will get a straight answer.

Repairs

Dead outlets & switches

Outlets that have stopped working, switches that buzz, lights that flicker. Diagnosed and fixed rather than covered over.

Panels

Breaker & panel work

Tripping breakers, overloaded panels and upgrades, done to code with the circuit load actually worked out.

Lighting

Fixtures & fans

Ceiling fans, recessed lights, exterior and security lighting fitted and wired properly.

Wiring

New circuits & rewires

Adding a circuit for an appliance, a workshop, or replacing old wiring that has reached the end of its life.

Remodels

Kitchen & bath electrics

Outlets, GFCI protection and lighting for a renovation, coordinated around the other trades on site.

Troubleshooting

Fault finding

Power loss, warm outlets or a breaker that will not stay on. Traced to the actual cause instead of guessed at.
